intro1|If you have never 3D printed, here is the short version. A printer builds a plastic part one thin layer at a time, from a file called an STL. Two things decide whether it comes out right: the size, and which side sits down on the printer's bed. Anything that hangs out over empty air needs extra plastic underneath it, called supports, or it sags. Get either one wrong, and you find out an hour into the print.
intro2|Forge moves that check onto your desk. You ask for a part. A small AI model running on my own computer designs it. The glasses show it on your real desk, at the exact size it will print, with the spots that need supports painted red. Turn it, resize it, and send a print-ready file to the printer.
