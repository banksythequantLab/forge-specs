# Recording the demo (reproducible take)

The video is a screen recording of the Lens Studio preview, driven by the LEAF `demo_run` scenario so every take has the same beats.

1. `mcp-lib.ps1` — talks to the Lens Studio MCP (`.mcp.json`) from PowerShell: `Invoke-LsMcp -Tool MovePreviewCamera -ArgsJson '{...}'`. `mcp-tools.ps1` lists the tool names.
2. `rec.py` — 30 fps capture of a screen rectangle with dxcam (Desktop Duplication → ffmpeg/libx264); holds `ES_DISPLAY_REQUIRED` so the display cannot turn off mid-take. ffmpeg's gdigrab managed 4 fps on this laptop and ddagrab hung on the hybrid GPU.
3. `take.ps1 -Name take8` — selects the Colorful Home simulated room, refreshes the Lens, sets the seated pose, starts the recorder on the preview viewport, waits for the opening frame, moves to the lean-in pose and runs `demo_run` (`run-demo.ps1`), then stops when the scenario reports back. Lens Studio must be the foreground window and unobscured.
4. `auto-take.ps1 -First 8 -IdleToActivate 120` — records unattended: waits until the user has been idle two minutes, brings Lens Studio to the front (focus only), checks the display is on (`display-on.ps1`), records, and re-arms if anything touched the machine during the take. `poll.ps1 -Name auto` blocks until `auto.status.txt` leaves "waiting".
5. `sheet.ps1 -In take8.mp4 -Out take8.sheet.jpg -Every 1 -Start 18` — time-stamped contact sheet, to read the pinch time off the take.
6. `edit.py take8.mp4 <vo dir> forge-demo.mp4 22.5 "landed=7.3,flip=9.8,..."` — cuts the take to the narration segments (`service/tools/vo-segments.txt`, synthesized with `service/tools/vo-generate.sh`) from the pinch time and the `[Demo] +Ns` beat log that `DemoRun` prints (`RunAndCollectLogsTool mode=tail`), freezing, speeding up or slowly pushing in where the narration and the take differ, crops out the LEAF banner, and adds the title overlays.

Gotchas found on the way: a console launched through WMI pops up in front of Lens Studio and gets recorded (launch hidden); JSON passed through nested `powershell -File` calls loses its quotes (call the MCP in-process); the Lens Studio dock widget's `mapToGlobal` did not match the screen, so the viewport rectangle was measured from a screenshot; Desktop Duplication delivers nothing once the display has turned off (a take recorded after 7 minutes idle was 70 s of one frame); a fixed-wait `demo_run` pinched the tag while the clip was still designing on a busy box, so the scenario is event-driven now.
